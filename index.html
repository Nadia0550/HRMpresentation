Bob Dolan receives about 100 résumés.

Question 1 · Part A

Four selection methods, in order

01

Résumé screen

Matched against job requirements. 100 → 20

02

Interviews

First round cut the list in half. 20 → 10

03

Personality test

PDP ProScan with a benchmark from his top rep. 10 → 8

04

Track record

Named major accounts signed in 2 years. 8 → 4

Question 1 · Part B

Right idea, better order

Dolan’s order

Résumé→Interview→Personality→Track record

Efficient funnel, but the strongest evidence arrived last and the test did heavy filtering.

Our recommended order

Résumé→Track record→Structured interview→Sales role-play→References→Personality (support)

Job-related proof first, personality last as a tie-breaker.

Question 2 · Advantages

Why personality tests help

Objective data

Same test for everyone, less gut-feeling bias.

Success benchmark

Compared with a proven top performer.

Flags risk

Spotted people who might give in to every request.

Transparent

Candidates could discuss scores. None disagreed.

BenchmarkTwo flagged candidates

Question 2 · Disadvantages

Where it can go wrong

Tiny sample

Benchmark built from one top rep and the VP.

Faking

Candidates may answer how they think a salesperson “should”.

Unproven validity

No evidence here that scores predict sales results.

Fairness risk

Cutting two people on an assumption could be unfair or illegal.

Question 3 · Improvements

A better hiring roadmap

01

Structured interview

Same job-related questions, scored on a rubric.

02

Sales role-play

Customer demands a discount. Observe the response.

03

Reference checks

Confirm results with past clients and managers.

04

Validate the test

Track new hires’ sales for 12 months.

05

Combine scores

Weigh all tools together, never one alone.
